## 6. Known Limitations
- Clipping detection is estimate-based (full-scale peaks). Not forensic.
- Very short clips or pure test tones can produce partial LUFS results (fallback in place).
- Reference match on extremely similar masters can still score high even if one has intentional clipping.
- Large WAV stems consume significant RAM/CPU during librosa analysis.
- Metadata embedding on WAV is not reliable.
